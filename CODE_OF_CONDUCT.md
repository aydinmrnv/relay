# Code of conduct

Be decent to the people you work with here.

- Criticise the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and how you know.
- No harassment, no insults, no discrimination, no sexual attention, and no
  publishing of anyone's private information.
- Assume a question is asked in good faith, and answer it or leave it.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the
project is the subject.

If someone breaks this, write to support@nullstack.one. Reports are kept
private. The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block
people from the repository.
